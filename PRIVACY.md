# Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-22. Applies to Notevine for Android, alpha 0.9.0 and later.

Notevine is a note-taking app. Your notebooks belong to you. The app has no account system, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting service. The developer receives nothing from your tablet unless you send it yourself, for example in a bug report.

## What stays on your tablet

- **Notebooks, recordings, images and settings.** They are stored in Notevine's private storage. If you choose a folder during setup, Notevine also saves a copy of each notebook there.
- **Handwriting search.** Recognition runs on the tablet. The recognition model is downloaded from Google once, when you turn it on.
- **Microphone.** Notevine uses the microphone only while you record audio. Recordings stay on the tablet unless you sync, back up or share them.
- **Diagnostics.** Notevine keeps a small log of crashes and slow moments on the tablet. It holds no notebook content. You can save it as a file from Settings, and it leaves the tablet only if you attach it to a report. Read it before you attach it.

## Features that use the internet

Each of these is optional, except the daily update check, which you can turn off. Nothing else is sent until you turn the feature on or use it.

| Feature | What is sent, and to whom |
|---|---|
| Google Drive sync and backup | Your notebooks are encrypted on the tablet before upload. Google stores the encrypted files in your own Drive. Notevine's developer cannot read them. Only your devices and your recovery key can decrypt them. |
| Canvas LMS | Notevine connects to your school's Canvas site with the access you give it. Your Canvas token is stored encrypted on the tablet. |
| AI assistants in the sidebar | The sidebar opens the ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini website. What you type there goes to that service under its own privacy policy. Notevine does not send your notes to it. |
| Web videos | When you add or play a YouTube, Vimeo, Dailymotion, Loom, Streamable or TED video, the tablet loads it from that service. YouTube videos use the privacy-enhanced youtube-nocookie.com player. |
| Document scanner | Scanning uses Google Play services on the tablet. |
| Update check | Once a day, Notevine reads a small public file from GitHub to see if a newer version exists. GitHub receives your IP address and the app version, as with any web request. You can turn this off in Settings, Help and about. Updates download from GitHub only when you tap Download. |
| Bug reports and feedback | These open a form on GitHub. Reports on GitHub are public, so do not include private information. |

## Your control

- Delete a notebook, then empty the Bin, to remove it from the tablet. Deleted recordings are removed with their notebook.
- Turn off sync or backup at any time in Settings. You can also delete the cloud copy there.
- Uninstalling Notevine removes its private storage. Copies in a folder you chose stay there.

## Children

Notevine does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
