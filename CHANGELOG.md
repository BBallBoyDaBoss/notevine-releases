# Changelog

Every Notevine release, newest first. The app shows the same notes under What's new.

## alpha 0.9.5

_Send pages to your AI sidebar, 2026-11-24_

### New
- ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini in the sidebar can get what is on screen, or the pages you are looking at. You see the picture first, and nothing is sent until you tap Attach.
- Share pages with or without their annotations.
- Lasso something and send it to your assistant, or drag a new lasso screenshot into the sidebar.
- Drag selected text from an assistant onto your page.
- The layer toolbar shows your first pages and your base ink, so a new notebook has layers from the start. Existing notebooks keep their layers exactly.
- On Samsung tablets the pen hover circle is off at first, because they show their own pointer. Settings, Pen has a switch.

### Improved
- Hold to straighten accepts rounded corners from fast drawing and lines that go past the start.
- Hold to straighten also straightens zigzags and other lines with sharp corners.
- The pen hover circle stays away while you write.
- The eraser goes up to 100 mm, and in Infinite it keeps its size on screen at any zoom.
- A lasso selects an item once a tenth of it is inside.
- Two fingers zoom and turn the canvas with a soft start, so a pan stays a pan.
- The quick wheel unfolds along its arc, closes after 4 seconds without use, and no longer opens on a double-click of the pen button.
- The minimap shows all free-canvas writing, however small.
- Menus, popups and dialogs open and close with one motion.
- Assistant pages switch without overlap, resize once when you let go, and use less memory in the background.
- Notevine asks before it arranges document layers as clusters. Opening a notebook never moves its pages.

### Fixed
- Ink zoomed out in Infinite no longer turns soft and thick while you write.
- Weekly tasks on Home sign in again through your school by themselves when the school remembers this tablet, and refresh while Home stays open.
- A held shape in Infinite no longer disappears for a moment.
- Live ink runs past sharp bounces, as in h, m and n, less.
- Page areas that could wait for a PDF picture until you scrolled are now told when it arrives.
- A picture is sent only to the assistant's own page, never to a sign-in page or another site.

## alpha 0.9.0 rev 6

_Support page, Help home and a bigger example notebook, 2026-11-23_

### New
- Support Notevine is a new Settings tab, and a link appears at the end of setup. Tips on Ko-fi help fund development and unlock nothing.
- The example notebook is now a full Biology 101 course with pages grouped into clusters on the canvas. It opens after setup.
- Help shows how Canvas works with your weekly tasks.
- Help has a Help home entry at the top of the topic list and a home button in the top bar. Either one returns to the home screen in one tap.

### Improved
- The guided tour cards in Help fill the full width and match the article cards.
- Folder and note cards on Home rise slightly when you hover over them.

### Fixed
- Help opens on its home screen at the top after the app restarts or updates. It keeps your place only while the app stays open.

## alpha 0.9.0 rev 3

_Tours, updates and taskbar fixes, 2026-11-22_

### New
- When a new version is out, a card shows what changed, with Download now and a progress bar.
- Tours for the extensions (Calculator, Canvas, Files with drag and drop, and the equation and symbol libraries) and for making ink and document layers.
- With the quick wheel on, double-click the pen button to go back to the pen.

### Improved
- Guided tours have a new card, and the screen is no longer dimmed, so every panel works during a step.
- What's new has its own place in Settings and a new look. The update card matches it.
- Toolbar settings are now part of General.
- With a pinned taskbar, Notevine stays above it, and the layer toolbar centres in the space you can see.

### Fixed
- Tours no longer stop after the first step in a notebook that syncs.
- The tour's select step let you pick the lasso but not circle a word.
- The tour card could sit under the taskbar.
