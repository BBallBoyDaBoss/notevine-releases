# Changelog

Every Notevine release, newest first. The app shows the same notes under What's new.

## alpha 0.9.0 rev 6

_Support page, Help home and a bigger example notebook, 2026-11-23_

### New
- Support Notevine is a new Settings tab, and a link appears at the end of setup. Tips on Ko-fi help fund development and unlock nothing.
- The example notebook is now a full Biology 101 course with pages grouped into clusters on the canvas. It opens after setup.
- Help shows how Canvas works with your weekly tasks.
- Help has a Help home entry at the top of the topic list and a home button in the top bar. Either one returns to the home screen in one tap.

### Improved
- The guided tour cards in Help fill the full width and match the article cards.
- Folder and note cards on Home rise slightly when you hover over them.

### Fixed
- Help opens on its home screen at the top after the app restarts or updates. It keeps your place only while the app stays open.

## alpha 0.9.0 rev 3

_Tours, updates and taskbar fixes, 2026-11-22_

### New
- When a new version is out, a card shows what changed, with Download now and a progress bar.
- Tours for the extensions (Calculator, Canvas, Files with drag and drop, and the equation and symbol libraries) and for making ink and document layers.
- With the quick wheel on, double-click the pen button to go back to the pen.

### Improved
- Guided tours have a new card, and the screen is no longer dimmed, so every panel works during a step.
- What's new has its own place in Settings and a new look. The update card matches it.
- Toolbar settings are now part of General.
- With a pinned taskbar, Notevine stays above it, and the layer toolbar centres in the space you can see.

### Fixed
- Tours no longer stop after the first step in a notebook that syncs.
- The tour's select step let you pick the lasso but not circle a word.
- The tour card could sit under the taskbar.
