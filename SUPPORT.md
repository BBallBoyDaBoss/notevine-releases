# Install, update and keep your notes safe

## Requirements

- An Android tablet with Android 12 or newer. Phones work, but Notevine is designed for tablets with a pen.
- A 64-bit ARM processor (almost every tablet sold since 2019).

## Install

1. Open the [latest release](https://github.com/BBallBoyDaBoss/notevine-releases/releases/latest) on your tablet.
2. Download the `.apk` file under **Assets**.
3. Open the file. If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps, then tap **Install**.

To check the file before you install it, compare its SHA-256 checksum with the one in the release notes.

## Update

Notevine checks for new versions once a day. Open **Settings, Help and about, What's new and updates** to see the notes and tap **Download update**.

- The app checks the file's size, checksum and signature before Android's installer opens.
- The first time, Android asks you to allow Notevine to install apps.
- Updating keeps all your notebooks and settings.

You can also install a newer `.apk` from the releases page over the old one. Never uninstall first: uninstalling deletes Notevine's private storage.

## Back up your notes

Use at least one of these.

- **A folder on the tablet.** In **Settings, Library and files, Storage**, choose a folder. Notevine saves a `.noteful` copy of each notebook there after you edit it. Copy that folder to a computer or a USB drive from time to time.
- **Google Drive backup.** In **Settings, Sync and backup, Cloud sync and backup**, turn on backup. Backups are encrypted on the tablet before upload. Save your **recovery key** somewhere safe: without it and without an approved device, a backup cannot be opened.
- **Export.** From a notebook's menu, export it as a `.noteful` file (complete, editable) or as a PDF (for sharing).

## Move to a new tablet

1. On the old tablet, make sure your Drive backup or folder copy is up to date.
2. Install Notevine on the new tablet.
3. Restore in one of these ways:
   - Connect the same Google account in **Settings, Sync and backup** and restore the backup.
   - Copy your `.noteful` files to the new tablet and open them with **Import**.

## Coming from another app

Notevine imports Goodnotes, Notability, Notewise, Samsung Notes and OneNote. Open **Import, Bring your notes** and follow the steps for your app. Anything that could not be transferred is listed when the import finishes.

## Recover deleted notes

Deleted notebooks go to the **Bin** in the library first. Open the Bin to restore them. Emptying the Bin deletes them for good.

## Get help

- In the app, tap **Help** for guides and short guided tours.
- Found a bug? In **Settings**, tap **Report a bug**. The form fills in your app version and tablet model.
